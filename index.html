About the author

I’m Chris Wang, an acoustic and vibration engineer. I write about the measurement and diagnosis of sound and vibration across vehicles, machinery and buildings, and about how those sounds are experienced.

Introduction

Making Sense of Sound is a technical writing series that connects these real-world experiences with acoustic and vibration engineering. Rather than treating individual metrics, methods and standards as separate topics, the series follows four connected perspectives:

  • The Receiver how people hear, perceive and respond to sound.
  • The Path how acoustic and vibration energy propagates, couples and reaches the receiver.
  • The Measurement how signals are captured, processed, interpreted and validated.
  • The Space how rooms, buildings, outdoor environments and context shape the final experience.

Together, they form a practical engineering sequence:

  1. Understand the experience →
  2. trace the physical mechanism →
  3. measure the evidence →
  4. improve the system or space

Author Background

I am a senior acoustic engineer with 8+ years of experience across automotive, machinery and household appliances, specialising in acoustic measurement, signal analysis, structural dynamics and sound quality. This series also extends that engineering perspective to building and environmental acoustics.
