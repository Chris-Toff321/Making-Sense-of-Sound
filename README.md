# Making-Sensen-of-Sound
Making Sense of Sound is an ongoing technical series exploring how sound is generated, transmitted, measured, perceived and controlled.
Rather than treating decibels or single-number ratings as complete answers, the series follows acoustic energy from its source, through airborne and structure-borne transmission paths, to the receiver and human perception. The initial articles cover hearing sensitivity, equal-loudness and A-weighting, sound transmission, building sound-insulation metrics, and the differences between laboratory and field performance.
The planned series expands into signal integrity, 
- acoustic metrics,
- psychoacoustics,
- source–path–receiver analysis,
- environmental acoustics
- building acoustics
- soundscape
- Each article connects physical principles, measurement methods and standards with practical engineering interpretation.
